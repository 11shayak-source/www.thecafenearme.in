Why Periodontal Health Matters

Gum disease (Periodontitis) is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults. It is a silent bacterial infection that destroys the gums and the alveolar bone supporting your teeth. If your gums bleed while brushing, or if you notice receding gum lines, immediate intervention is required.

At Aesthetica Roots, we employ advanced laser-assisted periodontal therapy. This minimally invasive technique vaporizes infected tissue inside the gum pockets and stimulates the regeneration of healthy bone and ligament, often preventing the need for traditional flap surgery.
