Comprehensive Dental Treatments

Laser Treatment

Modern dentistry relies on advanced laser technology to provide minimally invasive solutions. Laser dentistry minimizes bleeding, reduces swelling, and often eliminates the need for anesthesia during soft tissue procedures. From gum contouring to precise cavity preparation, our laser protocols ensure a significantly faster healing time and a more comfortable patient experience.

Dental Exams & Digital X-Rays

Preventative care starts with precise diagnostics. We utilize high-resolution digital X-rays that emit up to 90% less radiation than traditional film. Combined with thorough clinical examinations, our doctors can detect microscopic decay, bone loss, and structural abnormalities long before they become painful emergencies.

Root Canal Treatment (Endodontics)

When the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected, a root canal is necessary to save the natural tooth structure. Dr. Shourjya Sen and Dr. Pratik Sanyal utilize rotary endodontics to meticulously clean and seal the root canals. This pain-free procedure is highly effective in curing severe toothaches and preventing systemic infections.

Teeth Cleaning & Periodontics

Professional prophylaxis (teeth cleaning) removes hardened plaque and tartar that daily brushing cannot reach. Furthermore, our periodontal therapies address gum disease through scaling and root planing, restoring the foundation of your smile and preventing premature tooth loss.

Tooth / Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Whether dealing with severe decay, overcrowding, or impacted wisdom teeth, our extraction protocols prioritize patient comfort. Using advanced surgical techniques, we ensure rapid removal with minimal trauma to surrounding tissues, followed by comprehensive post-operative care instructions.

Tooth Fillings & Restorations

We use highly durable, tooth-colored composite resins and ceramic materials to restore cavities. These fillings are seamlessly blended to match your natural enamel, providing structural integrity while maintaining the aesthetic brilliance of your smile.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation & Cosmetics

For patients suffering from extensive dental issues, Full Mouth Rehabilitation combines multiple restorative and cosmetic treatments (including crowns, bridges, implants, and orthodontics). We engineer a holistic treatment plan to rebuild your bite, improve facial aesthetics, and restore full chewing function.
