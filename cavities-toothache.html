Understanding the Pain.

A toothache is rarely just a temporary inconvenience; it is your body's alarm system signaling that bacteria have breached the protective layers of your tooth.

Stage 1: Enamel Demineralization

The outermost layer is compromised. Usually painless, this stage requires preventative care.

Stage 2: Dentin Decay

Decay reaches the softer, sensitive layer beneath the enamel. Deep tooth-colored fillings are required.

Stage 3: Pulp Infection (Pulpitis)

The bacteria have reached the inner nerve center. A Root Canal Treatment is absolutely necessary to save the tooth.
